# Character reference ledger

Status: **TASK-035 analytical record; production guidance, not production prose**

## Boundary and method

These works are used only to compare abstract craft dimensions: conversational tactic, relation to authority or uncertainty, contradiction, humor boundaries, and ways of teaching or apologizing. No Second Current character is an adaptation of a source character. Writers must not copy source wording, names, plots, biographies, identity traits, catchphrases, or a complete personality; request writing “in the style of” an author; or imitate a performer or later adaptation.

The United States Copyright Office explains that a work enters the public domain when its copyright term expires in its [copyright lifecycle overview](https://copyright.gov/history/copyright-exhibit/lifecycle/). Each edition below is the Project Gutenberg text linked in the table; its ebook page identifies that edition as public domain in the USA. The project uses no quotations from these editions. Public-domain status here is a permission boundary, not evidence that copying would produce original or continuity-safe characterization.

## Reference constellations

<!-- reference-constellation:ev_shaw sources:eliot-middlemarch,wharton-age-innocence -->
### Ev Shaw

- *Middlemarch*: use the abstract tension between reforming an institution and discovering that knowledge alone does not confer authority.
- *The Age of Innocence*: use attention to coded rules, social cost, and what a careful speaker leaves unsaid.
- **Blend and transformation:** Ev's operational responsibility, Failure Analysis history, compact questions, and protected pilot are original to Second Current. The two dimensions combine institutional idealism with pressure-aware restraint; neither source supplies her biography, identity, plot, or exact diction.

<!-- reference-constellation:inez_calder sources:austen-pride-prejudice,nesbit-railway-children -->
### Inez Calder

- *Pride and Prejudice*: use quick social inference, lively correction, and the risk that an accurate observation supports a premature conclusion.
- *The Railway Children*: use practical attention to transport, objects, routes, and care expressed through useful action.
- **Blend and transformation:** Inez's Inflow authority, custody discipline, packaging memory, herbs, and futsal are original. The blend supports speed plus self-correction without importing a source romance, family, class setting, or verbal signature.

<!-- reference-constellation:malik_okoye sources:grahame-wind-willows,doyle-sherlock-adventures -->
### Malik Okoye

- *The Wind in the Willows*: use delight in apparatus, energetic demonstration, and the need to bound enthusiasm with consequences.
- *The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes*: use methodical observation and the separation of a clue from the conclusion a person wants it to prove.
- **Blend and transformation:** Malik is neither a reckless hobbyist nor an infallible detective. His test-profile authorship, generous teaching, rescue dog, label jokes, and reproducibility blind spot create an original professional contradiction.

<!-- reference-constellation:sora_chen sources:doyle-sherlock-adventures,eliot-middlemarch -->
### Sora Chen

- *The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes*: use comparison, attention to discriminating details, and conclusions that should be traceable to observations.
- *Middlemarch*: use the gap between principled ambition and the human or institutional effects of pursuing it too narrowly.
- **Blend and transformation:** Sora rejects genius theatrics. Her fault trees, operationally costly Isolation standard, paper folding, cooking, and astronomy form a new character whose skill is explicit method rather than source-character mannerism.

<!-- reference-constellation:luis_ortega sources:alcott-little-women,burnett-secret-garden -->
### Luis Ortega

- *Little Women*: use care expressed through practical craft, domestic competence, patience, and pride that can coexist with self-correction.
- *The Secret Garden*: use attentive maintenance, cultivation, and the idea that durable restoration requires conditions rather than a dramatic gesture.
- **Blend and transformation:** Luis's board-repair ethics, stop conditions, hidden process debt, family table, peppers, and fixture favors are original. Do not borrow a source family structure, childhood, disability narrative, or moralizing register.

<!-- reference-constellation:hana_park sources:austen-emma,austen-pride-prejudice -->
### Hana Park

- *Emma*: use confident judgment, social consequence, and the difficult practice of recognizing when one's helpful certainty needs correction.
- *Pride and Prejudice*: use calibrated wit, explicit re-evaluation, and conversational precision under status pressure.
- **Blend and transformation:** Hana's independent Gate authority, evidence euphemism list, swimming, orchestra bookkeeping, and sampling contradiction are original. Her wit targets language and process, never a learner's identity.

<!-- reference-constellation:bea_morgan sources:nesbit-railway-children,montgomery-anne -->
### Bea Morgan

- *The Railway Children*: use spatial and logistical attentiveness, communal problem-solving, and care for consequences beyond the visible handoff.
- *Anne of Green Gables*: use imaginative naming, social warmth, and a vivid relationship to place balanced by the need to correct overreach.
- **Blend and transformation:** Bea's dispatch practice, private destination map, dance class, postcards, and friendly rivalry with Inez are original. Avoid source names, orphan or family plots, rural setting, and ornamental speech.

<!-- reference-constellation:priya_nayar sources:wharton-age-innocence,forster-room-view -->
### Priya Nayar

- *The Age of Innocence*: use coded institutional speech, duty to audience, and the distance between an acceptable account and a privately known truth.
- *A Room with a View*: use pressure between direct feeling and social convention, especially when clearer speech carries a cost.
- **Blend and transformation:** Priya's public-infrastructure contract role, audience-first translation, museum mornings, practical shoes, and temptation to smooth bounds are original. No source relationship, social class, travel plot, or period voice transfers.

<!-- reference-constellation:jonah_reed sources:austen-emma,doyle-sherlock-adventures -->
### Jonah Reed

- *Emma*: use intervention meant as help, confidence shaped by incomplete context, and the possibility of candid self-correction.
- *The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes*: use indexes, linked observations, and systematic reconstruction while preserving the difference between evidence and display.
- **Blend and transformation:** Jonah's Rigline past, SIFT stewardship, defensiveness, transit maps, board games, and provenance ordering are original. He is not a mastermind and SIFT is not a character.

## Originality and review safeguards

1. Begin from TASK-034's semantic payload, not from a source scene or quotation.
2. Choose the Second Current relationship and pressure register before applying any reference dimension.
3. Combine at least two dimensions, then express them through the character bible's original work, routines, and blind spots.
4. Reject any line recognizable because of archaic syntax, a source catchphrase, plot echo, signature metaphor, or single-character imitation.
5. Read samples without speaker names. Recognition should come from reasoning tactic and relationship, not accent, identity shorthand, catchphrase, or repeated jargon.
6. Recheck dignity, player neutrality, hidden-information bounds, and source-independent comprehensibility before production use.

No source wording is approved for production. Later public-domain research must add a dated source row and a new constellation note before it can influence this guide.
