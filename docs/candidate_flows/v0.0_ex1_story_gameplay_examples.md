# Candidate flow v0.0 ex1 — Story-to-gameplay handoff

> **Status: explicitly non-authoritative example fixture.** This walkthrough tests one possible campaign presentation around the Candidate-Frozen Example Profile. It does not freeze story canon, campaign state, dialogue, rewards, AI behavior, UI architecture, or rules. Read it with the [example decisions](v0.0_ex1_decisions.md), [board, cards, decks, and Ticket fixtures](v0.0_ex1_board_and_cards.md), and [complete card-game replay](v0.0_ex1_cards_gameplay_examples.md).

Every `EX1-*` handle is local to this package and non-contractual. The technical truth of *The Quiet Cascade* remains reserved.

Where this story surface names score, it instantiates the frozen causal rubric. The required actionable Isolation and necessary matching-Repair records remain pending until closure; a valid close costs zero Actions, settles them, records closer statistics, and awards no closer point.

## Narrative context

At 05:42 in Trinity Hub's Inflow area, Mara begins a Continuity Rotation shift with three Civic Atlas returns. The machines do not share one public diagnosis:

- **Another DIMM, Same Slot** arrives after memory work and still does not POST.
- **No Offers Left** has link and IPv6 presence but no expected IPv4 lease.
- **Ready for Rebuild** is a degraded three-member SAS RAID 5 whose Bay 1 member is initially implicated.

The lot matters because a prior return record uses the weak phrase `Unit tested good`, one serial has appeared before, and the client-provided site history conflicts with an Inflow route record. Those story facts are authorized context, not hidden Fault Evidence and not a solution to any Ticket. Each Ticket's exact player-visible setup and public candidate set comes from the [board fixture](v0.0_ex1_board_and_cards.md#six-ticket-fixtures). Its answer and unused outcome branches remain `SERVER_ONLY`.

Mara wants to carry the intake context through Diagnosis, complete a cooperative three-Ticket shift, and leave Gate with a record another technician can trust. A computer technician occupies the second cooperative seat so the example can audit AI participation without granting an oracle.

## Flow SG-1 — Cold open to authoritative match handoff

1. **Enter the cold open.**
   - **Surface:** `SCR-COLD-OPEN`, `05:42 — Inflow, Trinity Hub`.
   - **Scene:** Inez stands beside three return cases. She identifies an overlaid route label, a repeat serial, and a site-code disagreement. The record she opens ends with `Unit tested good` but does not name a sufficient Verify condition.
   - **State and visibility:** these are authored campaign observations available to Mara and the cooperative cell. No Ticket answer, unexecuted outcome, private hand, or server candidate truth is present.
   - **Clock:** no match turn or player clock has started. Reading, captions, and animation consume no authoritative time.
   - **Motion:** scene layers use the shell's `M-01` destination transition; the identity portrait persists.
   - **Reduced motion:** replace the scene immediately, focus the scene heading, and provide all environmental facts in text.

2. **Choose a response attitude.**
   - **Dialogue modal:** Mara may respond **Direct**, **Curious**, **Careful**, or **Dry**.
   - **Example choices:** “Open the prior record,” “Which route is sourced?”, “Flag both histories,” or “So the labels disagree more precisely than the notes.”
   - **Result:** tone and a relationship annotation may change, but all four paths preserve the same authoritative context. No response creates Evidence, score, a Qualification honor badge, or technical truth.
   - **Accessibility:** **Skip scene** opens a factual recap and requires acknowledgement before continuing; skipping never removes actionable context.

3. **Receive Ev's actionable handoff.**
   - **Scene:** Ev states that the three Tickets are ready for a Continuity cell and asks Mara to separate what is observed from what is merely expected.
   - **Narrative result:** the campaign checkpoint advances to `Shift brief available`. This is account/campaign state, not match state.
   - **Failure recovery:** if the scene asset fails, **Text recap** supplies the exact source-labeled facts and advances only after the authoritative checkpoint save succeeds.

4. **Open the Shift brief.**
   - **Surface:** `SCR-SHIFT-BRIEF` inside `Shift`.
   - **Visible regions:** current shift, active three-unit lot, rotation badge, legal deck summary, story threads, and **Begin work**.
   - **Ticket facts:** the screen repeats each Ticket's visible symptom, public candidate set, explicit Verification/Gate success conditions, and the example-only possible pending categories—deepest Isolation and matching Repair—without displaying the answer. It states that closure costs zero Actions and awards no point itself.
   - **Malik's contribution:** “A pass answers the conditions we ran. Gate needs the conditions listed here.” This explains scope; it does not predict a pass.
   - **Motion:** `M-01`; reduced motion replaces the stage and focuses `Shift brief`.

5. **Inspect admissible authority context.**
   - **Drawer:** `DRW-AUTH-CONTEXT` groups records under **Client claim**, **Inflow observation**, **Prior Worklog**, **Ticket fixture**, and **Gate requirement**.
   - **For every record:** show source, author or system, event time, publication time if later, audience, raw/result summary, and whether it is scenario context or match Evidence.
   - **Visibility:** all entries shown here are authorized for Mara's campaign cell. The drawer does not list concealed result counts or placeholders for hidden outcomes.
   - **Motion:** an authorized record may enter the appropriate context list through `M-06`; it never flies from a hidden server area.
   - **Reduced motion:** insert it beneath a labeled visibility heading and announce its source.

6. **Audit AI assistance before play.**
   - **SIFT panel:** `DRW-SIFT` is visibly **Unavailable in this campaign slice** with the explanation `Learn Evidence and Isolation without recommendation ranking first`.
   - **Computer technician note:** Seat B will receive the same `TEAM` Evidence as Mara during cooperative play, its own hand, and public state. It chooses only among actions legal in that projection.
   - **Boundary:** neither SIFT, the computer technician, a story character, a spectator projection, accessibility text, nor Motion may read a Ticket's `SERVER_ONLY` answer.
   - **Help behavior:** control help may explain the public profile and current visible state. It may not recommend a candidate from hidden data.

7. **Review the prior Worklog.**
   - **Drawer:** `DRW-RECORDS` opens the prior `Unit tested good` record.
   - **Interpretation:** the interface labels it `Verify condition not specified`; it does not upgrade the sentence into a pass for POST, memory, DHCP, RAID health, or anything else.
   - **State:** viewing an authorized record is free and outside match Actions. Closing the drawer returns focus to the brief.

8. **Inspect preparation.**
   - **Decks:** Mara selects the legal 30-card `EX1-DECK-BENCH-HARDWARE`; deterministic campaign replay uses `EX1-SHUFFLE-G1-A` and `EX1-HAND-G1-A`.
   - **Teammate:** Seat B uses `EX1-DECK-SYSTEMS-FIELD`, `EX1-SHUFFLE-G1-B`, and `EX1-HAND-G1-B`.
   - **My Info:** `EX1-QUAL-FIRST-LOOK-SAFETY` appears only as an earned recognition honor badge; cosmetic state has no mechanics. Neither participates in preparation or legality.
   - **Legality check:** both decks are exactly 30, maximum card count 2, and contain no persistent card. Both match Benches will begin empty.
   - **Motion:** deck edits, if needed, use `M-04`; reduced motion updates list and validator text without moving focus.
   - **Failure recovery:** an invalid or unsynchronized deck returns Mara to Decks with the exact error. Begin Work cannot repair or auto-select a deck silently.

9. **Open Begin Work confirmation.**
   - **Modal:** `MOD-BEGIN-WORK` reconciles `EX1-GAME-1`, cooperative mode, exactly three Tickets, both seats/decks/variants, five-card opening hands, three Search Tokens, one Refresh Token, no turn timer, no player clock, and `Q = 0`.
   - **Ticket queue:** `EX1-TICKET-SFP012-NO-POST`, `EX1-TICKET-SFP030-NO-DHCP`, and `EX1-TICKET-SFP057-RAID-CASCADE`.
   - **Warning:** `Underlying Faults and unused authored outcomes remain unknown.`
   - **Authority request:** `StartCampaignMatch(episodeCheckpoint, EX1-GAME-1, seatAssignments, deckRevisions, expectedCampaignRevision)`.

10. **Recover from a stale checkpoint or deck snapshot.**
    - **Possible rejection:** a deck, account, content, or checkpoint revision is stale.
    - **Result:** the server returns the current player-safe revision and a named incompatibility; it does not return the hidden reason a card might be useful.
    - **Recovery:** install the safe snapshot, revalidate the deck, and reopen the same confirmation. No match, opening hand, or Worklog exists yet.
    - **Motion:** `M-14`; reduced motion focuses the error summary with no nudge.

11. **Synchronize honestly.**
    - **Surface:** `SCR-SYNC` checks account, campaign checkpoint, card content, both exact deck fingerprints, deterministic variants, player-safe match snapshot, local/remote engine, and both seats.
    - **Motion:** `M-13` advances only from actual check results.
    - **Reduced motion:** text checklist and progress only; no decorative spinner.
    - **Failure recovery:** Retry a failed check, return to preparation, or cancel before the match. A missing computer seat is not silently replaced with hidden-authority automation.

12. **Enter the match.**
    - **Surface:** `SCR-MATCH`; the primary dock collapses to Worklog, connection, accessibility/settings, and Leave/Concede.
    - **Authoritative setup:** only now are opening hands, draw stacks, starting Actions-on-turn, Search Tokens, Refresh Tokens, Ticket states, and revision instantiated.
    - **Clock:** gameplay becomes actionable after synchronization and the factual recap. Narrative reading and decorative settling remain outside authoritative time.
    - **Handoff:** follow `EX1-GAME-1` in the [complete card-game replay](v0.0_ex1_cards_gameplay_examples.md). The story layer does not add actions or results.

## Flow SG-2 — Match presentation without narrative omniscience

This section identifies the story-facing surfaces used while the separate game transcript supplies every exact turn.

1. **Observe and revise Hypotheses.**
   - Public candidate trays list only the authored candidates. Mara and Seat B may revise a two-candidate `TEAM` marker without a truth response.
   - `M-07` gently reorders only from Evidence already visible to the team. Reduced motion replaces the order and names the visible result that caused the revision.

2. **Create team Evidence.**
   - Accepted Test and Command actions create immutable events. Detailed results go to the cooperative `TEAM` tray until Documentation creates a public projection.
   - `M-06` moves Evidence only to its authorized surface. Reduced motion inserts it under `TEAM` and announces actor, Ticket, and source.
   - Seat B may cite team-visible Evidence because the rules permit it; this is collaboration, not hidden AI access.

3. **Commit accountable Isolation.**
   - The actor selects a public candidate and cites visible Evidence. Only acceptance publishes the isolated Fault and citations.
   - `M-08` settles an accepted Isolation into the Ticket-owned panel. Reduced motion locks it, moves focus, and announces fault plus citation count.
   - A rejection returns only `ISOLATION_NOT_SUPPORTED`, spends the Action under the profile, and reveals no wrong-versus-insufficient distinction.

4. **Separate Repair from proof.**
   - Accepted Repair changes the machine-state strip after public Isolation; it does not mark Verify, Documentation, or closure complete.
   - `M-09` crossfades before/after machine state and names changed fields. Reduced motion replaces the state and shows a textual Changed fields list.

5. **Present failed Verify as a return, not a rewind.**
   - On `Ready for Rebuild`, the first RAID Verify fails after the Bay 1 replacement and rebuild attempt. The Bay 1 change, attempted rebuild, failed result, and chronology remain.
   - The Ticket becomes **Returned to Diagnosis** and exposes only the new Bay 3 warning legitimately created by that Verify.
   - `M-10` pins prior Repair history and draws a route back to Diagnosis. Reduced motion shows a Returned to Diagnosis banner and preserved-history summary.
   - Seat B and SIFT receive no hidden advance knowledge of this result.

6. **Enrich the Worklog without rewriting time.**
   - Document Live and the closure bundle enrich original placeholders in sequence position and add a current publication event.
   - A successful final Verify opens a zero-Action closure window before automatic end-turn. Closure has statistical attribution, but no closer Service Point.
   - `M-11` links action time and publication time; reduced motion inserts both timestamps immediately and never reorders the earlier action.

7. **Reconnect deterministically if needed.**
   - On disconnection, actionable controls disable. Reconnect installs Mara's latest player-safe snapshot, then settles only explicit unseen semantic events; duplicate IDs are ignored.
   - `M-13`; reduced motion uses a text checklist. A stale action is rejected through `M-14`, does not move a card, spend an Action, or add a Worklog entry.

## Flow SG-3 — Gate review, consequence, and progression

1. **Finish the complete closure transaction.**
   - **Trigger:** the third valid zero-Action closure bundle in `EX1-GAME-1` settles the frozen pending causal records, records closure attribution without a closure point, then completes archive, replenishment, queue, and terminal checks.
   - **Result:** the cooperative queue is clear. The story does not declare success from a green animation before this result.

2. **Open Gate review.**
   - **Surface:** `SCR-GATE-REVIEW`; actionable clocks are stopped.
   - **Per Ticket sections:** Observed, Isolated depth, Repaired, Verified, Documented, Remaining uncertainty, and contributors.
   - **Another DIMM, Same Slot:** review the known-good comparison, accepted failed-DIMM Isolation, replacement, POST pass, and authored full-memory pass.
   - **No Offers Left:** review client-side/link context, pool audit, accepted pool-exhaustion detail, cleanup, and detailed lease/connectivity pass.
   - **Ready for Rebuild:** review Bay 1 Isolation and Repair, the preserved failed Verify, Bay 3 confirmation and Isolation, second Repair/rebuild, and passing RAID-health Verify in original order.
   - **Motion:** `M-15` reveals these sections after the authoritative final state exists.
   - **Reduced motion:** show all sections immediately and focus the result heading.

3. **Hear Hana's outcome-responsive review.**
   - **Dialogue:** Hana distinguishes what was repaired from what was proved and names any Documentation gap from the actual Worklog.
   - **Choice:** Mara may answer Direct, Curious, Careful, or Dry. The response changes tone only; it cannot rewrite the record or score.
   - **Boundary:** Hana cites released records. She never announces a deeper root cause than the accepted fixture supports.

4. **Play the after-shift scene.**
   - **Surface:** `SCR-AFTER-SHIFT`, Outflow.
   - **Scene:** Bea links the repeat serial to an earlier shipment route; Inez confirms the conflicting site code was not resolved by a label overlay. Ev asks Mara to preserve both facts for the next shift.
   - **Thread-forward result:** the campaign records an unresolved repeat-return/route thread. It does **not** state whether a supplier lot, test profile, field condition, process defect, client omission, or other cause explains *The Quiet Cascade*.
   - **SIFT:** remains disabled and contributes nothing to the conclusion.

5. **Apply progression separately from technical truth.**
   - **Modal:** `MOD-PROGRESSION` shows Continuity Rotation progress, a Malik consultation becoming available, the authoritative campaign checkpoint, account rewards defined by the full journey, and the archived Worklog link.
   - **Boundary:** story checkpoint progression may make a later consultation scene available. A Qualification may record the completed rotation as recognition only; it grants no method, catalog, Ticket, procedure, deck, or story access. Neither changes the completed match or reveals a future Ticket answer.
   - **Failure recovery:** if account reward synchronization is delayed, the match result and Worklog remain saved. Shift shows **Reward sync pending** and retries by idempotent result ID; it never replays the match.

6. **Archive and return to Shift.**
   - **Surface:** `DRW-RECORDS` shows the closed campaign Worklogs separately from the domain reference library and multiplayer history.
   - **Motion:** later record publication still uses `M-11`; shell return uses `M-01`.
   - **Reduced motion:** update records immediately, then focus the Shift heading.
   - **Exit state:** all three Tickets closed; original event order preserved; cooperative contributions attributable; campaign checkpoint saved; repeat-return thread unresolved; SIFT disabled; Quiet Cascade technical truth not established.

## Failure and recovery matrix

| Failure | Preserved state | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold-open art, portrait, voice, or animation unavailable | Source-labeled narrative facts and checkpoint. | Use text recap/captions; no loss of admissible context. |
| Authorized context record unavailable | Prior checkpoint and preparation. | Block Begin Work, retry source synchronization, or return to Shift. |
| Deck invalid or not synchronized | Saved legal deck and campaign checkpoint. | Open Decks, repair legality, save, select, then reopen confirmation. |
| Stale campaign or account revision | Latest safe campaign/account state. | Install snapshot, revalidate, submit a new intent. |
| Computer technician not ready | No match or hands instantiated. | Retry seat synchronization or return to preparation; do not grant it server access. |
| Match disconnect | Authoritative match and chronology. | Install player-safe snapshot, settle unseen events by ID, resume legal turn state. |
| Stale match action | Card, Action, Worklog, and score unchanged. | Accept safe revision, read the intervening public/team event, choose again. |
| Gate/result account reward pending | Closed match, score, Worklog, and checkpoint. | Retry idempotent reward synchronization; do not replay closure. |
