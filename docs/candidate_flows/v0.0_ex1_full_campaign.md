> **Status: explicitly non-authoritative example fixture.** This landing-to-sign-out journey is a reviewable product hypothesis, not frozen canon, a production UI contract, an account/security design, an AI design, or approved game balance. It applies only the temporary [Candidate-Frozen Example Profile v0.0](v0.0_ex1_decisions.md) and the exact [board, cards, decks, deterministic hands, account recognition/appearance fixtures, and Tickets](v0.0_ex1_board_and_cards.md).

The focused [deckbuilding](v0.0_ex1_deckbuilding_examples.md), [account appearance and recognition](v0.0_ex1_equipping_examples.md), [story handoff](v0.0_ex1_story_gameplay_examples.md), and [card-game replay](v0.0_ex1_cards_gameplay_examples.md) documents provide deeper audits. This file repeats the decisive state and arithmetic so the journey remains readable by itself.

Every `EX1-*` handle is a non-contractual replay label. *The Quiet Cascade* remains unresolved, SIFT remains disabled, and **Verify** remains the gameplay-stage term. Gate is the fictional quality organization, not a seventh rules department.

## Journey premise

Mara creates `EX1-ACCOUNT-MARA`, completes first-run identity and accessibility setup, receives a small example starter collection, and begins a Continuity Rotation shift. Their campaign cell must close exactly three Civic Atlas Tickets:

1. `EX1-TICKET-SFP012-NO-POST — Another DIMM, Same Slot`;
2. `EX1-TICKET-SFP030-NO-DHCP — No Offers Left`; and
3. `EX1-TICKET-SFP057-RAID-CASCADE — Ready for Rebuild`.

Mara occupies cooperative Seat A. A player-safe computer technician occupies Seat B. Together they use the two exact G1 deck permutations, clear the queue, review the released Worklogs at Gate, preserve an unresolved repeat-return clue, perform optional account management, and sign out.

## Product and authority assumptions used here

| Surface or state | Example assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Signed-out entry | `SCR-LANDING` sits outside the application shell. |
| Signed-in Home | `Shift` is Home and the brand target. Primary dock: Shift, Rooms, Decks, My Info, Store; Social is a drawer. |
| Nested destinations | Records is inside Shift. Recognition, Appearance, and Stats are My Info tabs. |
| Account authority | Currency, owned cards/cosmetics, saved/selected decks, Qualification honor-badge history, profile recognition, appearance, preferences, campaign checkpoint, and Room membership update only after accepted account results. |
| Match authority | The UI sends typed intents with expected revisions and reads player-safe snapshots plus semantic presentation events. |
| Narrative time | Cutscenes, dialogue, factual recaps, preparation, Gate review, and decorative transitions occur before the actionable clock begins or after it stops. This fixture has no turn/player clocks anyway. |
| Match shell | During play the dock collapses to Worklog, connection, accessibility/settings, and Leave/Concede. Store and routine Social alerts wait until results. |
| AI | The computer technician sees its legal Seat B projection. SIFT is disabled and supplies no hint, rank, publication, or Isolation. |

Coverage conventions keep the step register concise without leaving an implied request or animation:

- A step that names only navigation, tab selection, inspection, dialogue attitude, preview, or drawer open/close is local and sends no authoritative mutation request. Its result is the visible local destination or selection; authoritative account and match state remain unchanged.
- Unless a narrower audience is named, signed-in collection, balance, selected deck, recognition/appearance, preferences, and campaign checkpoint are `PRIVATE_PLAYER`; story facts explicitly released to the campaign cell are visible to that cell; only `PUBLIC_MATCH` state is spectator-safe.
- A shell or story destination change without a more specific cue uses `M-01`. Its reduced-motion equivalent is immediate replacement, focus on the new heading, and a route announcement. A local tab/drawer change is immediate, traps focus where modal, and returns focus to its trigger when closed.
- The failure branch local to a step is stated there when it changes the immediate choice. The journey-wide matrix records the preserved state and recovery for failures shared by several steps. No rejected or unknown request is presented as committed.

### Ledger conventions and presentation

`H/D/X/B` means Hand / draw Deck / discard / Bench. Each accepted action or resolution event creates the next public Worklog placeholder with actor, card/basic action, Ticket, public target surface, cost, and action time. Detailed Test results are `TEAM`; accepted Isolation, public Repair summary, Verify pass/fail summary, and Documentation projection are `PUBLIC_MATCH`. Hidden Ticket answers remain `SERVER_ONLY`.

The explicitly example-local causal rubric—not unresolved `SCORE-001`—records one pending point for the first eligible deepest Isolation and one for its necessary matching Repair. Neither changes score until a valid Document Close. The active Player receives an immediate post-final-Verify close window even with no Actions left; close costs zero, settles the eligible pending rows, records a closer statistic worth zero SP, archives the Ticket, replenishes resources, and ends the turn.

Every match intent carries a unique intent ID and expected revision. The authoritative result commits before presentation:

- accepted card movement uses `M-05`; reduced motion moves it instantly and announces cost, result, and destination;
- team Evidence uses `M-06`; reduced motion inserts it under the `TEAM` heading;
- Hypothesis revision uses `M-07`; reduced motion updates its text with no truth glow;
- accepted Isolation uses `M-08`; reduced motion locks and announces the cited public record;
- Repair state change uses `M-09`; reduced motion shows the Changed fields list;
- failed Verify uses `M-10`; reduced motion shows Returned to Diagnosis plus preserved history; and
- Documentation uses `M-11`; reduced motion inserts action/publication times without reordering.
