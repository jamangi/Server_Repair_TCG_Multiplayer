# Candidate flow v0.0 ex1 — Store, equipment, and appearance

> **Status: explicitly non-authoritative example fixture.** This walkthrough exists to test a complete account-management flow. It does not approve commerce, currencies, equipment mechanics, qualifications, cosmetics, inventory structure, or UI behavior. Read it with the [Candidate-Frozen Example Profile v0.0](v0.0_ex1_decisions.md) and the [example board, cards, decks, Tickets, and account fixtures](v0.0_ex1_board_and_cards.md).

All `EX1-*` handles below are local replay labels, not stable IDs or public data contracts.

## What this example is testing

The example asks whether the application can make six different ideas legible without quietly merging them:

| Concept | Meaning in this example | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualification | Campaign-earned permission to use a reviewed method or eligible preparation. | Not a card, purchasable perk, equipped item, or claim of expertise. |
| Owned card | A copy in the account collection that may be used in a legal saved deck. | Not automatically in a deck, hand, or match. |
| Deck entry | A count of one owned card definition in a saved 30-card deck. | Not equipment or a physical inventory slot. |
| Equipment | Account-owned preparation committed to an Equipment slot before Ready. | Not a deck card and not automatically a match-time Installed asset. |
| Installed asset | A match-time Bench object created only when explicit card or equipment text says so. | Not every item shown on the character. |
| Cosmetic | Account-owned appearance with no rules effect. | Not qualification, equipment, deck legality, or technical power. |

The two owned equipment fixtures below have **no effect in the audited matches**, never seed an Installed asset, and do not change the opening hand, Actions, Search Tokens, Refresh Tokens, Evidence, scoring, or Ticket legality. That absence is explicit rather than implied.

## Narrative context and starting account state

Mara (`EX1-ACCOUNT-MARA`) has completed First Look safety orientation and is preparing for a Continuity Rotation shift. They want a smaller bench caddy for the next scene and a signal-blue jacket for their portrait. Before changing anything, they inspect whether either choice affects their two saved example decks.

The account begins with this private state:

| Fixture | Account state | Current effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `EX1-QUAL-FIRST-LOOK-SAFETY` | Earned qualification. | Permits the compact caddy in this UI fixture; it does not alter cards or match rules. |
| `EX1-EQUIP-STANDARD-BENCH-CADDY` | Owned and equipped. | Mechanical-preparation category; no audited-match effect and never seeds Installed. |
| `EX1-EQUIP-COMPACT-DIAGNOSTIC-CADDY` | Owned and unequipped. | Requires the safety qualification; no audited-match effect and never seeds Installed. |
| `EX1-EQUIP-ADVANCED-REWORK-CADDY` | Catalog-visible, unowned, and qualification-locked. | Error fixture only; it cannot be committed. |
| `EX1-COSMETIC-SIGNAL-BLUE-JACKET` | Catalog-visible and unowned. | Appearance only; no mechanical effect. |
| Credits / Parts | `1,240 Credits`; `85 Parts`. | Example soft currencies only; no real-money purchase exists. |
| Saved decks | `EX1-DECK-BENCH-HARDWARE` and `EX1-DECK-SYSTEMS-FIELD` are legal. | Neither deck depends on these equipment or cosmetic fixtures. |
| Match Bench | Installed row empty. | Account equipment has created no match object. |

Every account request below carries an example intent ID and expected account revision. Preview actions are local and reversible; purchase, Equip, Unequip, and appearance changes are authoritative account requests.

## Flow EQ-1 — Locate, compare, replace, and undo equipment

**Player desire:** Mara wants to compare the compact caddy with the standard one without risking a hidden deck change.

**Plan:** inspect Qualifications first, preview the owned replacement, compare every affected state, commit it, audit the result, and then exercise a real Undo request.

1. **Open `My Info` from `Shift`.**
   - **Screen:** `SCR-SHIFT` changes to `SCR-MYINFO`; the persistent portrait, name, and rotation badge remain the same.
   - **Intent and result:** navigation is local. No account request is sent and no preparation state changes.
   - **Visibility:** identity, owned items, and qualifications are `PRIVATE_PLAYER`; only Mara's public portrait selection is shareable elsewhere.
   - **Motion:** semantic event `shell.destination_changed` uses `M-01`: the central stage fades and shifts 8 px while the dock and portrait persist.
   - **Reduced motion:** replace the stage immediately, move focus to the `My Info` heading, and announce the route.

2. **Inspect the `Qualifications` tab.**
   - **Screen:** `TAB-QUALIFICATIONS` shows `EX1-QUAL-FIRST-LOOK-SAFETY` as **Earned**, with source `First Look orientation` and a plain-language access statement.
   - **Intent and result:** tab selection is local. The qualification is read from the latest account snapshot; viewing it does not consume or equip it.
   - **Boundary:** the tab says, “Qualifications permit reviewed access. They are not cards, purchases, equipment, or proof that a diagnosis is correct.”
   - **Failure recovery:** if the qualification record is still synchronizing, the Equipment commit control remains unavailable and offers **Retry account sync**; it does not guess eligibility.

3. **Open the `Equipment` tab.**
   - **Screen:** `TAB-EQUIPMENT` separates the committed slot from the owned inventory. The standard caddy is labeled **Equipped**; the compact caddy is **Owned · Compatible**; the advanced caddy is **Not owned · Qualification locked**.
   - **State:** both saved decks remain **Legal**. A separate line reads `Audited-match Installed assets: none`.
   - **Accessibility:** ownership, compatibility, and slot state use text and icons as well as color.

4. **Preview the compact diagnostic caddy.**
   - **Screen:** selecting `EX1-EQUIP-COMPACT-DIAGNOSTIC-CADDY` opens a watermarked `PREVIEW` panel.
   - **Intent and result:** `PreviewEquipment` is local. The committed slot and account revision do not change.
   - **Comparison:** the panel shows Standard -> Compact, the satisfied qualification, both decks still Legal, Installed row still Empty, and audited-match effect `None`.
   - **Motion:** `M-12` shares the selected item into the preview and changes the portrait's tool context without treating the preview as committed.
   - **Reduced motion:** replace the preview immediately and show a before/after table; keep focus on the selected item.

5. **Choose `Equip`.**
   - **Modal:** `MOD-EQUIP-REPLACE` identifies the occupied slot and shows the complete replacement summary.
   - **Player choice:** Mara selects **Replace standard caddy** rather than Cancel.
   - **Authority request:** `SetEquipmentLoadout(slot, EX1-EQUIP-COMPACT-DIAGNOSTIC-CADDY, expectedAccountRevision)`.
   - **Pending state:** the button reads **Committing**. The preview remains labeled; it is not treated as success early.

6. **Receive the authoritative success.**
   - **Result:** the account returns a new revision. Compact becomes **Equipped** and Standard becomes **Owned**.
   - **Unchanged state:** collection counts, both 30-card deck lists, any separate review-fixture setup, match resources, and Installed row do not change.
   - **Motion:** after success, `M-12` moves the compact caddy into the slot once. A `TOAST-UNDO` names the prior item.
   - **Reduced motion:** update the slot instantly, focus the success summary, and show the same before/after values in text.

7. **Audit deck compatibility rather than assuming it.**
   - **Screen:** the Equipment result lists `EX1-DECK-BENCH-HARDWARE — Legal` and `EX1-DECK-SYSTEMS-FIELD — Legal` from the new account revision.
   - **Boundary:** the UI did not edit either deck to make this true. It requested validation against unchanged deck entries.
   - **Failure recovery:** if either status cannot be refreshed, it reads **Legality check pending**, and Ready remains unavailable until synchronization succeeds.

8. **Use Undo.**
   - **Intent:** `TOAST-UNDO` submits a new `SetEquipmentLoadout` request that names `EX1-EQUIP-STANDARD-BENCH-CADDY` and the latest account revision.
   - **Result:** success equips Standard and returns Compact to Owned.
   - **Boundary:** Undo is an authoritative reverse action, not a visual rewind or deletion of the earlier account event.
   - **Conflict recovery:** if another account change advanced the revision, the request returns **Equipment changed elsewhere**. The UI reloads both items and reopens comparison; it never forces the old state over newer data.
   - **Motion:** the reverse move occurs only after success. Reduced motion updates the slot and announces `Standard bench caddy equipped`.

9. **Exit the flow.**
   - **Destination:** Mara returns to `Shift` through `M-01`.
   - **Exit state:** Standard is equipped; Compact is owned; both decks remain legal; the safety qualification remains earned; the Installed row remains empty; no match or Room state exists.

## Flow EQ-2 — Purchase, preview, and apply a cosmetic

**Player desire:** Mara wants the signal-blue jacket shown in the Store wireframe, but wants proof that purchasing it cannot change technical ability.

**Plan:** inspect the catalog record, make one explicit purchase, verify that it entered owned inventory without auto-equipping, and commit appearance separately.

1. **Open `Store`.**
   - **Screen:** `SCR-STORE` opens from the primary dock with Cards, Equipment, and Cosmetics categories. The balance reads `1,240 Credits · 85 Parts`.
   - **Intent and result:** navigation and category filters are local; catalog and balances come from the latest account snapshot.
   - **Motion:** `M-01`; reduced motion performs an immediate route replacement and heading focus.

2. **Select the signal-blue jacket.**
   - **Screen:** `EX1-COSMETIC-SIGNAL-BLUE-JACKET` enters the live preview with price `650 Credits`, owned count `0`, destination `Appearance inventory`, and the statement **No gameplay effect**.
   - **Local preview:** Mara's portrait shows the jacket under a visible `PREVIEW` watermark. Lobby, match, and public profile still use the committed appearance.
   - **Motion:** `M-12` shares the catalog item into preview.
   - **Reduced motion:** replace the portrait preview and announce the item, price, and non-mechanical status.

3. **Open the purchase confirmation.**
   - **Modal:** `MOD-PURCHASE` shows item, `650 Credits`, current balance, resulting balance `590 Credits`, owned count, and `Purchase does not equip`.
   - **Player choice:** Mara selects **Buy**, not Put off.
   - **Authority request:** `PurchaseItem(EX1-COSMETIC-SIGNAL-BLUE-JACKET, shownPrice, purchaseIntentId, expectedAccountRevision)`.

4. **Resolve an unknown network outcome safely.**
   - **Failure fixture:** the confirmation response times out after submission. The modal reads **Checking purchase** rather than enabling a second Buy.
   - **Recovery:** the client queries the authoritative ledger by the same purchase intent ID.
   - **Result A:** if the ledger contains the purchase, continue with the recovered success once.
   - **Result B:** if the server confirms no purchase occurred, restore Buy with the unchanged balance.
   - **Boundary:** the UI never deducts locally, creates two jackets, or asks animation state whether payment happened.
   - **Motion:** pending uses actual status text, not a decorative spinner. Reduced motion uses the same checklist text.

5. **Receive the recovered success.**
   - **Authoritative result:** owned jacket count becomes `1`; Credits become `590`; Parts remain `85`; the account revision advances.
   - **Unchanged state:** equipped caddy, qualifications, cards, decks, any separate review-fixture setup, and appearance remain unchanged.
   - **UI result:** the Store card reads **Owned** and offers **View in My Info** or **Keep browsing**. It does not say Equipped.

6. **Navigate to `My Info -> Appearance`.**
   - **Screen:** `TAB-APPEARANCE` shows the current committed jacket and the newly owned signal-blue option.
   - **Intent:** selecting the jacket previews it locally again.
   - **Comparison:** the table contains visual slot and colors only. A Mechanical changes row reads `None`.

7. **Commit the appearance.**
   - **Authority request:** `SetAppearance(EX1-COSMETIC-SIGNAL-BLUE-JACKET, expectedAccountRevision)`.
   - **Result:** after success, the same portrait updates in My Info, Shift, Rooms, lobby presence, and later results.
   - **Motion:** the shared identity uses `M-12` only after success; reduced motion swaps the portrait and announces the new appearance.
   - **Undo:** the success notice offers **Undo appearance**, implemented as a new authoritative request using the previous cosmetic, not a local rollback.

8. **Exit the flow.**
   - **Exit state:** Credits `590`; Parts `85`; jacket owned and committed; no technical or deck state changed; Standard caddy still equipped after EQ-1's Undo; no Installed asset exists.

## Flow EQ-3 — Locked item and Ready-state recovery

**Player desire:** Mara notices an advanced rework caddy and later tries to change preparation while already Ready in a Room.

**Why this flow exists:** an honest UI must distinguish `visible`, `owned`, `qualified`, `compatible`, and `currently changeable` instead of collapsing them into one disabled button.

1. **Inspect the locked advanced caddy.**
   - **Screen:** `TAB-EQUIPMENT` can show `EX1-EQUIP-ADVANCED-REWORK-CADDY` for comparison, but labels it **Not owned** and **Qualification locked**.
   - **Preview:** visual inspection is allowed and remains watermarked. The UI exposes no hidden match state or hidden Ticket suitability.

2. **Attempt a stale commit.**
   - **Failure fixture:** an old screen still exposes Equip, so Mara submits the item.
   - **Authority result:** `ITEM_NOT_OWNED_OR_QUALIFIED`; account state and slot remain unchanged. The response does not fabricate the missing qualification.
   - **Motion:** `M-14` gives one restrained nudge and focuses the inline explanation.
   - **Reduced motion:** no nudge; focus the error summary immediately.
   - **Recovery actions:** **View Qualifications**, **Show compatible owned equipment**, or Cancel. Mara chooses compatible owned equipment.

3. **Enter a Room and become Ready.**
   - **State:** the Room snapshots the selected deck and mechanical equipment at Ready, as defined by `EX1-UI-08`.
   - **Visibility:** other participants see Ready and public portrait state, not Mara's private inventory.

4. **Try to equip Compact while Ready.**
   - **Authority result:** `LOADOUT_LOCKED_WHILE_READY`. No slot, deck snapshot, readiness, hand, or match state changes.
   - **UI recovery:** the modal offers **Stay Ready** or **Leave Ready and edit**. It never silently clears Ready.
   - **Motion:** `M-14`; reduced motion focuses the reason.

5. **Choose `Leave Ready and edit`.**
   - **First request:** `SetReady(false)` succeeds and the lobby publicly updates Mara to **Not Ready**.
   - **Second request:** only then may Mara submit `SetEquipmentLoadout` with the new account revision.
   - **Result:** Compact equips; Room deck/equipment validation reruns; Ready stays false until Mara explicitly selects **Ready** again.
   - **Motion:** `M-03` announces readiness change, then `M-12` handles the accepted equipment change. Reduced motion updates both labeled states without pose or pulse.

6. **Exit the flow.**
   - **Exit state:** Compact is equipped; Room state is Not Ready until revalidated; no deck entry changed; no equipment became a card or Installed asset; locked caddy remains unowned.

## Failure and recovery matrix

| Failure | Preserved state | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualification snapshot unavailable | Committed equipment and decks. | Retry account sync; do not infer eligibility. |
| Item not owned or qualification missing | Currency, ownership, and equipped slot. | View Qualifications or filter compatible owned items. |
| Purchase response unknown | No local ownership or balance assumption. | Query ledger by the same intent ID before retry. |
| Account revision conflict | Latest authoritative account state. | Reload and reopen comparison; never force a stale Undo. |
| Deck validation pending after equipment commit | Saved deck contents and committed equipment. | Keep Ready unavailable until validation completes. |
| Mechanical change attempted while Ready | Ready snapshot, equipment, and Room state. | Explicitly leave Ready, edit, revalidate, then Ready again. |
| Cosmetic preview canceled | Previously committed appearance. | Discard local preview with no request. |

## Motion and reduced-motion audit

- `M-01` changes shell destinations while preserving identity; reduced motion replaces the route and moves focus.
- `M-03` presents an accepted public readiness change; reduced motion updates and announces the label.
- `M-12` distinguishes local preview from accepted purchase, Equip, Unequip, or appearance commitment; reduced motion uses immediate replacement and a before/after table.
- `M-14` presents rejection without changing state; reduced motion focuses the inline reason with no nudge.
- Currency, ownership, qualification, compatibility, Ready, and Equipped states always have text. Color, portrait pose, motion, and sound are never their only carrier.
- Decorative preview time never consumes an authoritative turn clock.
