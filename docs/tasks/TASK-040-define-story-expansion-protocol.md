# TASK-040-HIGH: Define the Story and domain expansion protocol

## Status

**Completed 2026-08-28.** Depends on completed TASK-039.

## Objective

Turn the user's proposed expansion train into a repeatable gated protocol: use measured gaps first, add real-world cases only where they create distinct teaching value, extend the domain without orphaning relationships, then build six new one-cutscene-plus-one-Match episodes.

## Required reading

Read completely before editing `AGENTS.md`, this task, TASK-004, TASK-009, TASK-014, TASK-024, TASK-026 through TASK-030, TASK-039 and its deliverables, the case-study method/backlog, schemas, frozen/unfrozen decisions, Builder/solvability reports, and content/version migration guidance.

## Protocol requirements

Create `docs/story/EXPANSION_PROTOCOL.md` with explicit entry evidence, owner, outputs, validation, stop conditions, and approval gates for:

1. coverage interpretation;
2. selection of uncovered existing playable arcs;
3. calculation of Q, the minimum additional qualifying real-world cases needed to supply six distinct solvable episodes after reusable existing arcs are counted;
4. source selection and lifecycle reduction;
5. candidate-object deduplication and nearest-existing-object analysis;
6. schema/rule/authority pressure review;
7. domain relationship impact and integration;
8. Ticket/fingerprint/Card/deck reachability and solvability proof;
9. six-episode graph/Match blueprint;
10. context-complete, characterized writing and choreography;
11. art inventory/production only where reusable assets are insufficient; and
12. versioned Story release, migration, accessibility, browser QA, and coverage re-audit.

Define Q as an output, not a target guessed in advance. A source may support multiple candidates, but six episodes must have distinct learning objectives and independently solvable Tickets. Prefer underused existing content when it forms a truthful complete lifecycle; do not add objects solely to make counts look balanced.

Separate Commands into three questions: catalog exposure, useful evidence-gathering role, and required Isolation role. Record the current absence of required Story Commands as an audit result. Do not redesign Commands inside this protocol task; any authority/rule change must enter the decision process and may be deferred without blocking unrelated expansion.

Define terminology: the expansion target is six player-facing episodes, each containing a story sequence plus one real Match. Script-file or act grouping may differ, but reports and UI must not call four containers and six Matches the same count.

## Verification

Walk one existing pilot case and one campaign-one Shift through the protocol without changing them. Verify every artifact handoff has a consumer, every mutation gate names its authority, Q is reproducible from TASK-039 inputs, and no stage permits web research to become domain/gameplay truth without review. Run documentation links and `git diff --check`.

## Allowed paths

Protocol/checklist documentation, task/index, approvals only when the protocol exposes a concrete lettered choice, and concise root/Story/case-study indexes. No research expansion, domain content, gameplay content, Story scripts, or Viewer code.

## Completion boundary

Stop when a future builder can expand the training corpus without guessing Q, duplicating concepts, skipping provenance, or mixing research, domain authority, gameplay solvability, writing, art, and release into one unreviewable change.
