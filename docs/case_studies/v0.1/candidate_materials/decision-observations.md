# Decision observations from the v0.1 pilot

These are research observations for user review. They neither add candidate decisions to the living list nor alter frozen or unfrozen rules.

## `CASE-OBS-001` — Negative verification can reopen Diagnosis

- **Evidence:** In `sfp-057`, replacing one failed member starts but does not complete a rebuild; the failed verification reveals a second failed member and sends the case back through Hypothesize, Test, and Isolate.
- **Question:** Does a failed Verify transition a Ticket back into Diagnosis, preserve the attempted Repair and Evidence, and create a new actionable fault context?
- **Dependencies:** `TST-001`, `REP-001`, `CROSS-004`, and the unfrozen consequences for failed repairs and verification.
- **Pressure:** The frozen Observe → Hypothesize → Test → Isolate → Repair → Verify → Document sequence must permit iteration without being mistaken for a one-way stage machine.

## `CASE-OBS-002` — Isolation needs evidence standards, not one universal test shape

- **Evidence:** `sfp-012` and `sfp-039` isolate by substitution; `sfp-030` by administrative inspection; `sfp-057` by report interpretation; `sfp-076` only by a qualified inference after a combined repair.
- **Question:** Which evidence classifications can confirm Isolation, and when must a player retain uncertainty or multiple causal candidates?
- **Dependencies:** `ISO-001`, `HYP-001`, `HYP-002`, and `TST-001`.
- **Pressure:** The unfrozen Diagnosis commitment threshold and Evidence requirements need to accommodate different diagnostic paths without treating repair success as automatic proof.

## `CASE-OBS-003` — Combined repairs weaken Root Cause attribution

- **Evidence:** `sfp-076` applies thermal paste, removes dust, and changes cable routing together. The system improves, but the source cannot cleanly assign causality to one intervention.
- **Question:** Can a multi-target or bundled Repair earn Isolation/Root Cause credit, and how does the engine represent confounded success?
- **Dependencies:** `ISO-001`, `ISO-004`, `REP-001`, and `TST-002` if the repair is followed by repeated tests.
- **Pressure:** Exact Root Cause Bonus requirements and consequences for unnecessary repair remain unfrozen.

## `CASE-OBS-004` — A repair action may simultaneously be a diagnostic substitution

- **Evidence:** The new DIMM in `sfp-012` and new PSU in `sfp-039` both generate strong Evidence and remain as the repair.
- **Question:** Does one authoritative execution create both a Test result and a Repair state change, or must the engine represent two linked atomic actions with one physical intervention?
- **Dependencies:** `TST-001`, `ISO-001`, `REP-001`, `DOC-002`, and action-cost rules.
- **Pressure:** Frozen server action serialization and Worklog projection require an unambiguous event model even when lifecycle functions overlap.

## `CASE-OBS-005` — Documentation evidence supports action-targeted publication

- **Evidence:** `sfp-030` records configuration and command transcripts; `sfp-057` attaches an ADU report generated by a specific diagnostic action.
- **Question:** When documenting an action, which attached inputs, outputs, interpretations, and later conclusions are published automatically?
- **Dependencies:** `OBS-001`, `DOC-002`, `DOC-003`, `DOC-005`, and `DOC-008`.
- **Pressure:** The frozen Worklog example can reveal action identity while withholding detailed Evidence; the candidate model may initially hide more and enrich the same chronological record later.

## `CASE-OBS-006` — Real resolution often omits explicit Documentation

- **Evidence:** `sfp-012`, `sfp-039`, and `sfp-076` reach reported resolution without a separate ticket/report action. `sfp-030` and `sfp-057` contain stronger evidence records during Diagnosis rather than only after Verify.
- **Question:** Is Documentation required for mechanical Ticket closure, optional but rewarded, or required only for selected actions/Evidence?
- **Dependencies:** `DOC-001`, `DOC-004`, `DOC-006`, `DOC-007`, `CROSS-001`, and `CROSS-002`.
- **Pressure:** Frozen Document-at-the-end wording and future unsynchronized items already distinguish conceptual order from incremental or optional publication.

## `CASE-OBS-007` — Verification must name the required operational state

- **Evidence:** `sfp-057` reports server restoration but not healthy RAID status; `sfp-076` reports 35 °C idle but no repeated load test; `sfp-030` says the cleanup worked without preserving the assigned lease.
- **Question:** What Evidence satisfies Verify: disappearance of the symptom, success of a fault-specific validation, restoration of service, or all applicable conditions?
- **Dependencies:** `CROSS-001`, `DOC-001`, `DOC-006`, current Validation Procedure semantics, and the unfrozen consequences for failed verification.
- **Pressure:** The existing candidate batch says Verify has no independent question, but these cases suggest a more fundamental verification-completeness decision may be needed before Ticket closure can be deterministic.

## `CASE-OBS-008` — Speculative repair can reveal a deeper causal chain

- **Evidence:** In `sfp-057`, the initial member replacement is reasonable and addresses one failure, yet rebuild failure reveals another bad member. In `sfp-076`, combined interventions prevent clean pre-repair Isolation.
- **Question:** When a Repair occurs before complete Isolation, can its result become Test Evidence, and what penalties or progress follow if it is partly correct?
- **Dependencies:** `ISO-003`, `REP-001`, `TST-001`, and `CROSS-004`.
- **Pressure:** A strict Diagnosis-to-Repair gateway may prohibit realistic but informative interventions; an unrestricted model may trivialize Isolation.

## `CASE-OBS-009` — Hypotheses may be useful without a generic conclusion object

- **Evidence:** Every pilot source reasons through named candidates, but only the Isolate step needs an actionable fault conclusion. The hypotheses are revised conversationally and often remain incomplete.
- **Question:** Which, if any, Hypothesis state must be authoritative for game effects, and can private player reasoning coexist with server-authoritative Evidence and Isolation?
- **Dependencies:** `HYP-002`, `TST-001`, `ISO-001`, and `CROSS-004`.
- **Pressure:** The unfrozen Diagnosis commitment can be narrowed or reassigned without inventing a generic “Player conclusion” entity or an eighth Diagnose action.
