# Decision and modeling observations from the Story expansion cases

These observations do not change Frozen Rules, Command semantics, schemas, domain objects, or gameplay content. Later owners may resolve them within existing authority or enter genuine pressure into the decision process.

## `EXP-OBS-001` — Existing domain breadth is not playable episode breadth

- **Evidence:** All six selected primary Symptom/Fault/Test/Repair/Validation chains already exist in the draft canonical domain, and all six primary Tests are in the Global Diagnostic Bench. None of the six Repairs or six Validations has a current response Card, and the six fingerprints/Ticket assemblies do not yet exist.
- **Observation:** Research completion must not be reported as six playable episodes. Later integration still needs six fingerprints, twelve response Cards, deterministic outcomes/routes, exact deck reachability, and solvability proof.
- **Authority:** Content and gameplay integration under existing Frozen Rules; no new rule implied.

## `EXP-OBS-002` — DSET probe plus clear spans Test and Repair

- **Evidence:** In `exp-004`, one DSET probe/log-collection operation refreshes or clears the lingering “backplane degraded” management state after current storage checks are healthy.
- **Observation:** The real action contributes both fresh diagnostic evidence and a state change. The current domain correctly separates `test.management.event_log_freshness` from `repair.management.clear_stale_alert_state`; a future Ticket must preserve that accountable boundary or explicitly escalate modeling pressure. A combined source action is not permission to let Test silently Repair.
- **Authority:** Frozen action/result and Repair-gateway behavior; stop later content if it cannot be represented without changing that authority.

## `EXP-OBS-003` — Command exposure does not establish use or requirement

- **Evidence:** `smartctl`, detailed IPMI event listing, and `ethtool` are globally exposed, but none of the selected sources proves execution of those current Commands. The predictive-drive source uses controller-management status, DSET is a vendor diagnostic utility, and the firmware cases use repair/management interfaces. None establishes a required Command action for Isolation.
- **Observation:** Every later report must retain separate catalog-exposure, useful-evidence, and required-Isolation columns. Do not promote a potential acquisition alternative into a required route or change Command semantics to manufacture coverage.
- **Authority:** Existing Command and typed Isolation contracts; no rule decision requested.

## `EXP-OBS-004` — Qualified physical repair must retain its fallback boundary

- **Evidence:** `exp-001` reports successful correction of bent socket contacts, while the existing `repair.compute.restore_socket_contacts` permits qualified contact work or replacement of the supported board FRU.
- **Observation:** Story/gameplay may teach inspection and the service decision without presenting delicate contact manipulation as universally safe or user-permitted. Source success does not erase ESD, magnification, torque, qualification, or replacement boundaries.
- **Authority:** Existing repair/protocol content and technical-safety review.

## `EXP-OBS-005` — Predictive failure is not failed-media replacement with a new label

- **Evidence:** The selected stronger `exp-003` source preserves an online drive's amber/green indication, management “Failure Predicted” evidence, planned replacement, and successful rebuild; it does not preserve a SMART attribute dump.
- **Observation:** The later fingerprint must preserve pre-failure data protection, exact-member identity, and rebuild/clean-predictive verification. Reusing an already-failed-drive outcome would erase the distinct learning objective even though much storage tooling overlaps.
- **Authority:** Existing distinct Fault/Test/Repair/Validation objects; content authoring and solvability review.

## `EXP-OBS-006` — Version and vendor evidence belongs in provenance, not universal rules

- **Evidence:** `exp-005` resolves after rollback to a particular iDRAC version, and `exp-006` uses a GA-7PESH2-specific BMC recovery path.
- **Observation:** Exact versions, filenames, utilities, and device messages must remain source/Ticket pins. Generic firmware compatibility and recovery objects may consume the cases only after provenance and applicability review; they must not claim those exact instructions work across platforms.
- **Authority:** Domain technical review and versioned content; no new domain object requested.

## `EXP-OBS-007` — Stale-alert closure needs evidence preservation and non-recurrence

- **Evidence:** In `exp-004`, healthy storage plus disappearance of the old alert after state refresh distinguishes stale management state from a failed backplane.
- **Observation:** “Clear the log” is not sufficient Repair or Verify by itself. The episode must preserve the prior evidence, establish current hardware state, refresh the obsolete state, reproduce/soak the complaint conditions, and require non-recurrence. This supports the existing no-trouble-found protocol rather than a speculative FRU replacement.
- **Authority:** Existing Test/Repair/Validation/Document contracts.
