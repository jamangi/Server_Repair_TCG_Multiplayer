# TASK-011 illustration art bible

Status: production contract for the 104 canonical V0 action and public-Symptom illustrations.

## Shared world

Every image belongs to one realistic late-night enterprise service environment: deep graphite and navy structure, cool cyan rack light, limited amber task light, controlled practical indicator color, and tactile metal, plastic, cable, instrument, and work-surface materials. The scene should feel cinematic because of composition and lighting, not because hardware becomes fantastical.

Use technically plausible hardware scale, connectors, airflow, drive form factors, cable routing, tools, antistatic practice, and service posture. No brands, logos, watermarks, weapons, combat language, anthropomorphic servers, fantasy circuitry, baked interface frames, Card borders, Ticket paper, family ribbons, labels, readable terminal commands, or pseudo-text. HTML owns titles, syntax, costs, state, and mechanics.

Images may establish an environment or method, but may not reveal a hidden Fault, predict an Evidence disposition, imply a Repair restored service, or certify a Verify result. Generated-Ticket panoramas depict only the public observed manifestation.

## Lighting and camera grammar

- Use one dominant practical light direction, cool rack ambience, and a smaller warm task-light counterpoint.
- Prefer close or medium-close technician viewpoints. Wide establishing shots are reserved for symptoms that genuinely need room context.
- Preserve plausible perspective and depth of field without hiding the technical subject.
- Keep screens dark and structured. Their rows, traces, blocks, or topology may communicate information shape, but generated glyphs must be abstract and unreadable.
- Avoid large areas of crushed black, neon bloom, and excessive teal-orange grading; overlaid HTML must remain readable over the crop.

## Crop contracts

Action masters are 1600×900 (16:9), delivered at 800×450. Keep the meaningful instrument, component, connector, or readout inside the middle 60% horizontally and the middle 70% vertically. Bench, hand, and Deck views may crop the top or bottom; no essential subject may live only at an edge.

Symptom masters are 2400×720 (10:3), delivered at 1200×360. Keep all critical observed information inside the centered 2.15:1 region (about 1548×720 in the master), while ambient racks, cabling, or room context may extend to the full width. The composition must also remain intelligible at a centered 3.35:1 crop.

## Family prompts

- Test: show the act of observing or exercising a system—a component, instrument, controlled setup, telemetry trace, inspection viewpoint, substitution, or inventory procedure. Do not choose a pass or fail result.
- Command: use a consistent dark service-console family with nearby hardware context. Vary logs, inventory, route, interface, memory, storage, and event-history information shapes without readable generated text.
- Repair: center the affected component and correction in progress. Hands and tools appear only when credible and safe. Do not show a final healthy state.
- Verify: show the repaired capability under neutral measurement or observation. Avoid checks, success colors, passing values, applause, or victory framing.
- Symptom: show only the observable manifestation and uncertainty. Never spotlight a hidden component or compose a candidate as the answer.

## Negative prompt grammar

Append to every generation request: no branding or watermarks; no readable text, numbers, labels, or terminal syntax; no UI chrome, Card frame, Ticket paper, family-color border, or cost medallion; no fantasy circuitry, combat, weapons, or anthropomorphic hardware; no impossible connectors, unsafe energized work, or casual live probing; no hidden answer, diagnostic outcome, Evidence disposition, future machine state, green check, or guaranteed success.

## Review gates

Each subject must pass technical recognition, family distinction, crop safety, tonal consistency, accessible alt text, hidden-information neutrality, provenance, dimensions, and byte-budget review. Contact-sheet review additionally rejects accidental duplicates, repeated focal compositions without documented justification, illegible generated pseudo-text, and a generic recolored-server treatment.

Safety-sensitive work receives a second check against the record’s authored note. If a safe procedure cannot be shown credibly, use a tool/component still life or a de-energized setup rather than inventing risky action.
